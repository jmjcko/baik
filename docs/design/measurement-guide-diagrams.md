# Measurement Guide Diagrams (Design Standard)

Applies to the visual guide modal `src/components/baikfit/MeasurementGuideModal.tsx`
(AGENTS.md section 6: every measurement explanation must have a CAD/vector diagram).

## 1. Visual standard

| Element | Rule |
| :--- | :--- |
| Figure | Frontal athlete line art, black outline (about 3 px at 1024), white skin |
| Kit | Dark grey cycling bib kit, fill `rgb(74, 77, 82)` (`#4a4d52`), same as `inseam_cad.jpg` |
| Baked-in marks | None. No arrows, ticks or labels inside the raster. All dimension graphics are SVG overlays |
| Canvas | Square 1024 x 1024 JPG, shown in a 420 px max square card; overlay SVG uses `viewBox="0 0 1024 1024"` so coordinates below map 1:1 to image pixels |
| Dimension vector | 4 px line with arrowheads, reticle (filled dot, dashed ring, cross) on every anatomical landmark |
| Callout | White rounded badge, bold label (`diagram...DimLabel`) plus grey sub-label (`diagram...DimSub`) |
| Text size | The 1024 px SVG is shown at roughly 410 px (scale 0.4). On-screen text must stay at least 11 px, so SVG font sizes are: badge title 30-32, badge sub-label 19-22, landmark labels 28-29, all with a white halo (`paintOrder="stroke"`) where they sit on the figure. Check the longest CS string still fits the badge (about 0.62 x font size per bold character) |
| Icons / emoji | None (AGENTS.md zero-icons rule). Pure line art and typography |

Colour per guide (so a rider recognizes the measurement at a glance):

| Guide | Colour | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Inseam | blue `#2563eb` | floor to top of book |
| Torso | teal `#0d9488` | book to sternal notch |
| Shoulder width (acromion) | blue `#2563eb` | acromion to acromion, handlebar match note |
| Arm length | violet `#7c3aed` | acromion tip to fist centre |
| Crank length | amber `#b45309` | BB spindle center to pedal spindle center, stamp callout |
| Saddle height (current bike) | cyan `#0891b2` | BB center to the 70 mm width point of the saddle along the seat tube |
| Handlebar width (current bike) | pink `#be185d` | center to center across hoods or drops |

## 3. Regenerating the images

```bash
python3 -m venv .venv-guides
.venv-guides/bin/pip install pillow numpy scipy
.venv-guides/bin/python scripts/guides/build_guide_images.py
```

`scripts/guides/build_guide_images.py` performs, in order:
1. erases the baked-in dimension arrow and sternal tick from the source,
2. flood-fills the bib straps, bib and shorts with the kit colour,
3. removes the original horizontal book and the crotch gusset (base image),
4. derives the three outputs: base (acromion), base plus vertical book (torso), base plus clenched fists (arm).

Re-run it after changing the source art or the style constants (`KIT`, `INK`).
`.venv-guides/` is a local tool environment and must not be committed.

## 4.1 Source of the guide texts

The steps and clinical notes follow `docs/knowledge/baikfit_kb_v1.1.0.md`: inseam (KB 2.1: barefoot in cycling shorts, heels 15 cm apart, thin hardcover book or carpenter's level pulled up with saddle-like pressure, LeMond 0.883, knee extension 30 to 35 degrees), shoulder width (KB 2.2: distal bony prominences of the scapula, not the deltoid, handlebar 1:1, men 390 to 410 mm, women 320 to 380 mm, the 420 mm factory trap), torso and arm (KB 2.3). The current setup guides (crank, saddle height, handlebar width) follow KB 2.4. `src/lib/engines/baikfit/__tests__/protocols.test.ts` fails when a guide text loses its KB key phrases or the KB loses the protocol facts.

## 5. Localization

Every text in the overlay comes from `translations.ts` (`wizard.diagram*`, `wizard.*Steps`, `wizard.*ClinicalNote`), with matching `en` and `cs`.
Never hardcode a label in the SVG. Landmark labels: `diagramAcromionLeft`, `diagramAcromionRight`, `diagramAcromionTip`, `diagramFist`.

## 7. Adding a new guide

Guides are configuration, not hand-written SVG (Story 4.4).

1. Create or derive a 1024 x 1024 image in the standard above (add the generator step to `build_guide_images.py`).
2. Add `src/components/baikfit/guides/<name>.ts` exporting a `GuideConfig`: `type`, `testId` (`<type>-cad-vector`), `image` (`src` with cache version and the translation key of the alt text), `markers` (arrow id and colour; the reverse marker `<id>Rev` is derived) and `elements` (`line`, `circle`, `rect`, `text` with React SVG prop names; text uses `textKey`, a key of the `wizard` translation block).
3. Register it in `guides/index.ts` (`GUIDES`, `GUIDE_TEXT_KEYS`, `GUIDE_STEPS`) and extend `GuideType` in `guides/types.ts`.
4. Add the texts (title, steps, clinical note, overlay labels) to `src/lib/i18n/types.ts` and both `en/wizard.ts` and `cs/wizard.ts`.
5. Add triggers (`GuideTrigger` in the wizard, the compact trigger in the pre-purchase view) and tests (the guides snapshot test covers every `GuideType`).
6. Update this document (colour table, inventory, coordinates).

## 8. Known limitations and next steps

- The vertical book (torso) and the fists (arm) are drawn programmatically and are intentionally schematic. They have no shading or perspective, unlike the 3/4 inseam render.
- Best next step for a uniform look: have one set of renders produced from a single prompt/brief (frontal and 3/4 pose, dark bib kit, white skin, thin black outline, no text or arrows, book vertical for inseam and torso, clenched fists for arm) and keep this document's coordinates as the contract for the overlays.
- The arm length is measured along a slightly bent arm in the steps text, while the diagram shows a straight vertical vector for clarity.

## 4. Discipline scenes (wizard step 1)

`src/components/baikfit/DisciplineDiagram.tsx` draws a minimal black and white line scene per riding discipline: road in perspective (road), mountains with a trail (MTB), rolling countryside with a track and gravel stones (gravel) and a city skyline with a house (trekking and urban). Style: thin monoline strokes (1.3 and 0.8 at a 240 x 100 viewBox), round joins, no fills, `currentColor` (slate-900), elegant and calm. The title is always the dominant element of the card (`text-lg`, bold); the scene is a quiet accent below the description (`h-12`, slate-400, left aligned). Pure line art without text and without icons; the SVG is `aria-hidden`. Earlier drafts with bike drawings were rejected by the product owner (a thin wireframe, then an outlined colour version). To add a discipline add one scene component and one entry in `SCENES`.

## 5. Report schematics on guide figures

The report schematics of the handlebar width, the crank length, the saddle height and the pitch and cleats are drawn on figure artwork in the guide style (white panel in both themes, `FigureFrame`, overlay colors and badges in `schematics/figureParts.tsx`). The artwork is never edited by hand; the overlay carries every number and every text (EN and CZ), so the artwork must stay free of text, arrows and grids.

| Schematic | Artwork (`public/assets/guides/`) | Origin | Landmarks (image pixels) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Handlebar width | `acromion_cad.jpg` | the existing guide figure | acromion (312,172) and (712,172), bar level y = 712 |
| Crank length | `crank_tdc.jpg` | generated externally from a brief (crank at 12 o'clock, no text); the generator's logo in the bottom right corner was painted white | bottom bracket (515,735), pedal axle (546,626), knee (704,268); the hip is outside the canvas |
| Saddle height and pitch | `saddle_side.jpg` | generated externally from a brief (saddle and post in side view, nose to the right); the logo corner was painted white | saddle top on the post axis (486,304), post axis bottom (592,742); the artwork is rotated 4.5 degrees in the pitch schematic so that the saddle is level |
| Cleat position | `cleat_sole.jpg` | generated externally (right shoe from below, toe up); the logo was painted white and the dashed guide lines the generator drew were mostly removed by a script, faint remains are visible | 1st metatarsal joint (660,339) on the inside edge, scale about 3.1 px per mm (shoe length 843 px), pedal axle notch (512,381) |

Briefs for new artwork: pure white background, thin black outline, white skin, dark grey (#4a4d52) kit, soft ground shadow, square 1024 x 1024, no text, no labels, no arrows, no dimension lines, no grid, no logo or watermark. Check the image for a visible logo before it is added (the licence of generated images is the product owner's responsibility). The overlay scales with the crank: the drawn crank length in pixels stands for the rider's crank length.
