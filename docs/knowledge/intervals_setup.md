# Intervals.icu: how a rider connects (Epic 51, story 51.4)

1. Connect your Garmin (or Wahoo, Polar and others) account to Intervals.icu in its Settings, Connections. It is free.
2. In Intervals.icu open Settings, Developer Settings and create an API key.
3. In bAIk open bAIkcare, Garage, Wear, the card Intervals.icu. Paste the key (the athlete id `0` means the owner of the key) and press "Save the key and load rides".
4. Choose which rides count for this bike: the bike of Intervals.icu, if your rides have one, and the types of ride (for example every road ride).
5. Press "Save and load rides". Later, press "Update rides".

The key stays in your browser and goes only to Intervals.icu. "Forget key" deletes it and the rides loaded from Intervals.icu. The key can read your data in Intervals.icu: do not paste it anywhere else, and make a new one in Intervals.icu if you think it leaked.
