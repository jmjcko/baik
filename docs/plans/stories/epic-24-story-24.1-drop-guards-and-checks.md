**QA Candidate**: Yes | **Author**: AIRE_PRODUCT_OWNER | **Status**: Done

## Acceptance Criteria

1. With numb hands neither the action plan nor the stock bike audit advises lowering the handlebars, moving the saddle forward or a longer stem, for any seed bike and size.
2. Neck pain and lower back strain never get a lowering advice either; an endurance style or an inflexible rider never gets one without a symptom.
3. The saddle tilt, the bar width, the cleats and the spacers are worded as checks, with the reason for the rider's symptom.
4. With numb hands the plan runs tilt, bar width, reach, drop, saddle height; neck pain adds the reach and drop checks; no spacer removal is advised on an assumed factory height.
5. Cases A, B and C of `docs/knowledge/saddle-to-bar-drop-rules.md` are unit tests; EN and CZ.
