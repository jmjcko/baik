## 👤 User Reference

**Description**: Crank guide: the length is stamped on the inside of the crank arm near the pedal thread (for example 172.5); fallback: measure from the center of the bottom bracket axle to the center of the pedal spindle. Saddle height guide: from the center of the bottom bracket along the seat tube line to the top of the saddle at the 70 mm width point (KB 1.1). Handlebar guide: center to center across the hoods or the drops.

**User Flow**: One journey: the rider opens step 2 of Tune My Current Bike, enters the current crank length, saddle height and handlebar width with the help of the guides, continues to the report and later to the pre-purchase audit; the values are stored in the profile.
